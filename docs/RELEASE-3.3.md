# 3.3.0

This update is about making it easier to see what to do next. No balance changes, your 3.2 worlds and configs still work.

## Menus
- The Hitching Post page shows who is docked and what to do next, with a button for each thing the Post does
- Island Projects shows the three projects as a path, and only the step you are on has buttons
- The Oathstone explains an oath in order: the skill, what you get now, what comes later
- The skills screen (K) is wider and shows the full perk names. Click a skill to see its perks
- Goals and Rewards show the crew's goal and three suggestions for what to do next
- `/skyblock` with nothing after it opens a menu

## HUD
- The goal shows a hint, how many challenges are left to the next tier, and rewards waiting to be picked
- Toasts no longer cut off their text

## Fish
- New textures for all 23 void fish
- In the Journal, fish you have not caught are grey and the one you picked is shown bigger
- Fine, Trophy and Mythic fish show a letter (F, T, M) in the corner of the slot

## Visitors
- Each visitor has a wares page: what they buy, what they sell and how much of it you carry
- The Rug page shows which cards are likely next
- Reputation is shown as numbers, with what the next tier gives
- When a visitor arrives you are told how many of their trades you can fill. Those trades have a green tick on the trade screen
- A bounty you carry enough for says (ready)

## Text
- More than 500 texts are shorter and clearer
- The guide book starts with First day, Generator layout and How a run works

## Fixes
- `/skyblock how iron ingot` now mentions smelting before the rare mob drop
- A finished goal is replaced by the next suggestion
- Lost and Found (if it is on) no longer lists slabs, stairs, walls, torches and crafting tables
